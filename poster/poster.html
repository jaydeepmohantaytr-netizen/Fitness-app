Aim & Introduction

  • Aim: one app that tracks workouts, habits and nutrition, and keeps users motivated with game-style rewards.
  • Problem: most fitness apps split these areas, need the cloud and do not personalise.
  • Our idea: a single dashboard with a local AI, so personal data stays on the device.

Conclusions

  • One app can combine habits, workouts and nutrition.
  • Streaks and multipliers reward consistency, not one-off effort.
  • A local AI keeps data private and costs nothing to run.

Applications

  • Students and school fitness programmes.
  • Coaches or gyms monitoring members (Coach panel).
  • Personal wellness and habit building at home.

Limits & future scope

  • Limits: AI needs a capable laptop; macro estimates are approximate; tested on a small scale so far.
  • Next: deeper coach tools, mobile app, progress history charts.
