Aim

Make healthy living simple. One app plans your workouts and meals for your goal and checks your body in five minutes.

How it works

  1. 1Enter your age, height, weight and goal.
  2. 2Get a workout and diet plan built for you.
  3. 3Take the checkup and track your progress.

Model / Prototype: the working app

Overview BMI, body view and progress

Workout a weekly plan for you

Diet meals, calories and protein

Checkup pulse, vision, hearing, reaction

Conclusions

  • One app can guide training, eating and health checks.
  • Plans adapt to goal, time and age.

Applications

  • Teens starting workouts at home.
  • Health-awareness booths at school events.

Limits

  • Educational screening only. It cannot diagnose.
  • Checkup needs a camera and speakers.
