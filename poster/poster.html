BMI, body view and weekly progress at a glance.

Pulse, steadiness, vision, hearing and reaction time, measured on the computer.

Aim & how it works

Aim: make healthy living simple. One app plans your workouts and meals for your goal and checks your body in five minutes. It also has an AI Doctor section.

  1. 1Enter your age, height, weight and goal.
  2. 2Get a workout and diet plan built for you.
  3. 3Take the checkup and track your progress.

Conclusions

  • One app can guide training, eating and health checks.
  • Plans adapt to goal, time and age, with safety tips for under-18s.

Applications

  • Teens starting workouts at home.
  • Health-awareness booths at school events.

Limits

  • Educational screening only. It cannot diagnose.
  • The checkup needs a computer with a camera and speakers.
